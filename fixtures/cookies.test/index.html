Tram extension to open next spring

The new tram line to the airport will open in April, two months later than first planned, after testing on the final section took longer than expected.

Services will run every twelve minutes during the day, with extra trams at weekends when flights are busiest.

The council says fares will match the rest of the network, and contactless payment will work from the first day.
